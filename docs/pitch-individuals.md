## Nobody plans to leave suddenly

You mean to write it all down one day: the accounts, the policies, who should have the flat, where the papers are. Sometimes there isn't time.

A father goes for his morning walk and doesn't come home. Within days, his family faces questions only he could answer. Which accounts? Who is the nominee? What did he want? What are the passwords?

It happens to the young too. At AIIMS Delhi, more than half of the sudden losses studied were people aged 18 to 45.[^young-in] And the money waits: **₹72,454 crore** sits unclaimed in Indian bank deposits.[^in] In Singapore, **2 in 5** people lost before 65 had never named a nominee for their CPF savings.[^sg]

## A true friend, for everyone

Most of us have one friend we call when something is hard to say out loud. You tell him, *"I want my daughter to have the flat."* He doesn't lecture you or take sides. He finds a few good lawyers and lets you pick. A month later he asks, *"Is the will done?"* And one day, when you are no longer here, he tells your daughter everything you wanted her to know, in your own words.

Not everyone has a friend like that. **Anvaya gives everyone that friend: Saathi**, a warm companion you simply talk to. Over a few relaxed conversations, Saathi gathers what your family will one day need. When the time comes, it is handed safely to the person you chose, and to no one else.

## How it works

1. **Talk.** Tap the microphone and speak in your own language, or type, or snap a photo of a policy. Saathi listens first. If you sound tired or sad, the practical questions wait for another day.
2. **Keep.** Small cards appear as you talk: a story, an account, a nominee. You tap **Keep** or **Not now**. Each card says only what you said. Saathi never guesses.
3. **Close the gaps.** "No nominee on this policy." "No will for the flat." Each gap becomes a simple next step. When you need a professional, Saathi shows a few vetted partners with their prices, and you choose. It never recommends one, and no partner can pay to be shown first.
4. **Lock your secrets.** Passwords stay locked on your own phone, for the one person you choose. Not Anvaya, not your bank, not Saathi can read them.
5. **Hand over, safely.** Your family uploads the official certificate. Careful checks and two different people confirm it. A waiting period follows, and one tap on your phone cancels everything if it's a mistake. Then the person you chose opens your vault, asks *"What insurance did Papa have?"*, and hears the answer in your words, with where it came from. They also get a checklist and draft claim letters for every bank and insurer.

## Watched over at every step

- **You hear first.** If anyone reports your passing, you are told straight away, and one tap stops everything.
- **Nothing risky is kept.** A password someone asked you for, or a payment someone else wants, never enters your book.
- **Your voice alone can't change anything important.** Changes need your own screen. Saathi is also learning your voice (being tested). When the voice on a call doesn't sound like yours, that card waits, and you confirm on the card that it was you before it is kept. So nobody else can keep anything in your name.
- **Another voice is noticed.** If someone joins your conversation, Saathi warmly asks who is there, and you decide calmly what to keep.
- **Our checks keep learning.** Every hour, we look for new tricks. What two independent sources confirm is added, and our team can remove anything.

## Vikram's story

> **Vikram, 58, Bengaluru**, tells Saathi about his first salary, which he gave to his mother. He mentions his LIC policy and asks that his nephew Ajay be the nominee. Saathi notices there is no will for the flat, and Vikram picks a will writer from three.
>
> Years later, Vikram leaves us suddenly. His daughter **Priya** is not ready; nobody ever is. But she is not lost. She asks *"What insurance did Papa have?"* and gets the policy, the nominee and a draft claim letter, all in her father's own words.
>
> For years, Saathi had been Vikram's friend. Now it was there for Priya.

## Our promises

- **You decide.** Only what you keep reaches your family. Nothing is shared with a partner until you agree.
- **No made-up answers.** If you never said it, Saathi says so.
- **Your real voice, never a copy.** We never clone a voice or build an avatar.
- **No paid influence.** Partners can't buy a better place.
- **No advice we aren't licensed to give.** Legal and financial advice comes only from licensed partners.
- **Yours to take with you.** What you keep is stored in an open, standard format.

## What it costs

A **simple yearly plan**, for one person or the whole family. Your first conversations are free. If your bank offers Anvaya, it may cover your plan. **Your family never pays to receive what you left them.**
