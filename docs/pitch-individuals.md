## Nobody plans to leave suddenly

You mean to sit down one day and write it all out: the accounts, the policies, who should have the flat, where the papers are. You tell yourself there is time.

Most of us do. And sometimes, there isn't.

A father goes for his morning walk and doesn't come home. A mother who ran the whole household is gone after a short illness. There is no goodbye, no talk at the kitchen table, no folder left on the shelf. And within days, a family still in shock is facing questions nobody prepared them for:

- Which bank accounts, insurance policies and investments were there?
- Who is the nominee? Where are the papers?
- What did they want for the house, the savings, the watch?
- What are the passwords?

The one person who knew every answer is the one person they can no longer ask.

And it isn't only something that happens late in life. Across Asia, sudden loss takes people in their 20s, 30s and 40s, the years when a family depends on them most:

- In India, more than half of the sudden losses studied at AIIMS Delhi were people aged 18 to 45, most of them "apparently healthy".[^young-in]
- In South Korea, more than 5,000 people aged 50 or younger have a sudden cardiac arrest each year.[^young-kr]
- In Malaysia, one in four people with an acute heart attack is under 50.[^young-my]
- In South-East Asia, road crashes are the leading cause of loss for people aged 5 to 29.[^roads-who]

The younger we are, the less we think we need to put things in order. Our families have the most to lose.

Across Asia, the money waiting to be claimed is staggering. In India, ₹72,454 crore sits in unclaimed bank deposits and nearly ₹9,000 crore in insurance.[^in] In Japan, a record ¥129.2 billion went to the national treasury in a single year because no heir came forward.[^jp] In South Korea, ₩10.3 trillion in insurance money waits to be claimed, and most families don't know it is theirs.[^kr] Malaysia holds RM13.3 billion in unclaimed money.[^my] In Australia, A$21.2 billion sits in lost and unclaimed superannuation.[^au]

And the sudden losses leave families least prepared. In Singapore, 2 in 5 people lost before the age of 65 had never named a nominee for their CPF savings.[^sg]

## Meet Anvaya

**Anvaya is a safe, secure way to pass on everything that matters to your family. At its heart is Saathi, Anvaya's warm companion, whom you simply talk to. Over a few relaxed conversations, Saathi gathers everything your family will one day need: your accounts, your nominees, your wishes and your stories. When the time comes, it is handed safely to the person you chose, and to no one else.**

No forms. No lawyer's office. Just a conversation, in your own language, whenever you have a quiet moment.

## What it feels like

- **Tap the microphone and just talk.** It feels like a phone call with an old family friend. Saathi answers out loud, in your language, and you can interrupt any time. If your phone blocks the microphone, Saathi tells you how to allow it, or you can simply type.
- **Saathi knows where to begin.** It starts with the people who matter to you and a story or two. Later it asks where things are kept. For your wishes, it waits until you are ready.
- **Saathi listens to how you feel.** If you sound sad, it simply listens, and the practical questions wait for another day. If you sound tired, it offers to stop. If you are enjoying a memory, it enjoys it with you.
- **Or type, or share a photo.** Snap your insurance policy and Saathi reads it for you.
- **You decide what is kept.** Small cards appear as you talk: a story, a piece of advice, a bank account, a family member. You tap **Keep** or **Not now**. Only what you keep becomes part of what your family will one day receive.
- **Your words, as you said them.** Each card says only what you said. If a detail is missing, Saathi leaves it empty and asks you later. It never guesses.
- **A book for your family.** Everything you keep becomes a book, with chapters for your stories, money matters, wishes and people. Each page shows the day you kept it, and the cover shows how ready it is.
- **Your conversation stays private.** It's held only so Saathi can follow along, with account and card numbers hidden. It's never handed over, and you can delete it whenever you like.
- **Keep your real voice.** For any story, you can keep the recording of you telling it, so one day your family hears it from you, not about you.
- **Leave voice letters.** Record a message for someone special or a moment you may not be there for: *"For Priya, on her wedding day."* It stays sealed until the time comes, and reaches only the person you chose.
- **Saathi notices what's missing.** "No nominee on this policy." "No will for the flat." You get simple next steps, each with a button. And when the moment is right, Saathi gently asks about the one thing that matters most: *"Who should receive the LIC policy?"* Say "skip", and it won't ask again.
- **Your secrets stay yours.** Passwords and account details are locked on your own phone. Nobody else can read them: not Anvaya, not your bank, not Saathi.
- **You choose who receives them**, usually a son, daughter or spouse. They accept in their own app, and you confirm a matching code.

## Help when you need it, never a sales pitch

When a next step needs a professional, such as a will writer or a safe place for original papers, Saathi shows you **vetted partners, with their prices up front**, and explains what each one does. You compare and choose. Saathi never recommends one over another, and no partner can pay to be shown first.

If your life spans more than one place, say a flat in Karnataka and savings in Singapore, Saathi notes where everything is and shows partners who can help in each place, so your family knows where to start.

If you choose one, and only if you agree, Saathi sends them a short summary so you don't have to start from zero. Your vault is never shared with anyone.

| Kind of partner | What they help with |
| --- | --- |
| Legal | Writing a will, registering it, succession certificates, probate |
| Safekeeping | Storing original papers, safe deposit lockers, digital document lockers |
| Your bank or insurer | Updating nominees, consolidating old accounts, claims |
| Claims help | Handling claims across many institutions for your family |
| Tax and accounts | The final tax return, closing accounts |
| Property | Transferring land and housing records |
| Licensed advisers | Financial planning, only from licensed partners |

## When the time comes, your family is not alone

- A family member uploads the **official certificate** from the registrar.
- Careful checks look for forgery or mistakes, and then **two different people** on our review team confirm it: one confirms the loss, the other confirms your family member's legal authority.
- A **waiting period** follows. If there has been a mistake, one tap on your phone cancels everything.
- The person you chose **unlocks your vault on their own phone**.
- They can **ask Saathi**, "What insurance did Papa have?", and hear the answer in your own words, with where it came from. If you never said it, Saathi says so honestly. It never guesses.
- They can **ask about anything you kept**, freely. If they mention someone asking them for a fee, Saathi gently adds that banks don't ask for fees on a call.
- Where you kept a recording, Saathi offers to **play your real voice** telling the story. And each **voice letter** reaches the person it was meant for.
- Saathi prepares a **checklist and draft claim letters** for every bank and insurer, and trusted partners are there for what comes next.

## Looked after at every step

Something this precious needs watching over, not just at the end, but all along the way.

- **You hear first.** If anyone ever reports your passing, we tell you straight away, with a link to cancel. One tap, and nothing moves.
- **Big changes take a breath.** Changing who receives your vault, or who your nominee is, takes a short pause and your own confirmation. If the change comes from a phone we haven't seen before, we check with you first.
- **Your voice alone can't change anything important.** We always ask you to confirm on your own screen, so even a copy of your voice could never take over.
- **Saathi notices another voice.** If someone else joins your conversation, Saathi warmly asks who is there. What was said then is marked, so you can decide calmly what to keep. Saathi never records anyone's voiceprint.
- **Nothing risky reaches your book.** Everything new is checked before Saathi offers it to keep. A password someone asked you for, or a payment someone else wants, is never kept.
- **No one can pressure you through us.** If it ever seems someone is pushing you to change your wishes, Saathi won't confront anyone. It simply holds the change, and checks with you privately, later, when you're alone. You stay in charge.
- **We watch ourselves too.** Two different people approve every handover. They can't pick which cases they review, and we keep an eye out for anything unusual in how our own team works.
- **A rule change reaches you, gently.** When a new rule affects you, such as India now letting you name up to 4 nominees for a bank account, Saathi tells you once, inside the app. It links to the official notice and never asks for an OTP, a password or a PIN. So a phone call about a "new RBI rule" is easy to recognise as not real.
- **Our checks keep learning.** The tricks people try change over time. Every hour, we look for new ones. What two independent sources confirm is added, our team checks the rest, and our team can remove anything.

## Vikram's story

> **Vikram, 58, Bengaluru**, opens Anvaya one evening and taps the microphone.
> *"My first job was at a textile mill in Mysore in 1987. I gave my whole first salary to my mother."*
> Saathi asks how his mother reacted. A card appears: **Story: First job and salary → Keep**.
>
> Later: *"I have an LIC policy ending in 4492. Please make Ajay the nominee."* Saathi adds the policy, asks who Ajay is ("my nephew"), and reminds him to submit the change to LIC too.
>
> He says the flat in Mysore should go to his wife, Meena. Saathi notices there is no will and shows three will writers with their prices. Vikram picks one.
>
> Years later, Vikram leaves us, suddenly, as he always feared he might. His daughter **Priya** is not ready; nobody ever is. But she is not lost. She asks *"What insurance did Papa have?"* and gets the policy, the nominee and a draft claim letter, all in her father's own words.
>
> Then she asks about his first job, and Saathi asks, *"Would you like to hear him tell it?"* And there is Vikram, in his own voice, talking about the salary he gave to his mother.
>
> He had left one more thing: a voice letter, *"For Priya, on her wedding day"*.

## Our promises to you

- **You decide.** Only what you keep reaches your family, and nothing is shared with a partner until you agree. You can delete your conversation at any time.
- **Yours to take with you.** What you keep is stored in an open, standard format, so it never depends on us alone.
- **No made-up answers.** If you never said it, Saathi says so.
- **Your words, as you said them.** Saathi never adds a detail you didn't say.
- **Your real voice, never a copy.** Your family hears the recordings you chose to keep and the letters you left. We never clone your voice or build an avatar of you.
- **No advice we aren't licensed to give.** Legal and financial advice comes only from licensed partners.
- **No paid influence.** Partners can't buy a better place or a recommendation.
- **Your secrets stay locked** until the right person, and only that person, receives them after careful checks.
- **Watched over all along the way.** We look out for anyone trying to misuse what you leave, at every step, and you always hear from us first.
- **Your family's privacy matters too.** Family members can ask to be added. While you are here, only you can let them in.

## What it costs

Anvaya is a **simple yearly plan**, for less than most of us spend on the small things we never think twice about, and a fraction of what a family can lose when nobody knows.

- **Try it first.** Your first conversations with Saathi are on us, so you can feel how it works before you choose a plan.
- **For you, or for the whole family.** Choose a plan for yourself, or one that brings your spouse and parents in too.
- **It may already be included.** If your bank or insurer offers Anvaya, they may cover your plan.
- **Partners set their own fees**, and you always see them before you choose.
- **Your family never pays to receive what you left them.** The handover, and a generous allowance of questions to Saathi, checklists and draft letters, are included. If they need more, they can add it later.

Download Anvaya from the app store, or look for it inside your bank's app. It is the same Saathi either way, and it records accounts at every bank and insurer, not just one. If your bank offers it, the accounts you hold with them can arrive already filled in, for you to confirm with **Keep**. Anything you hold elsewhere stays private, for your family. Your bank never sees it.
