**Keeping what matters**
- A photo or document Vikram shares now gets its own card, with his comment and what Saathi found in it. Once he keeps it, it is in his book under a new **Documents** chapter, and he can open it there. The items found in it, such as a policy, link to it, and the knowledge map shows the link.
- After the handover, Priya sees "Documents Papa kept", with his words about each, and can open them. Nobody else can, and nobody can before the handover.
- Discarding a conversation deletes its photos and documents that were not kept. Removing a kept document deletes the file too.
